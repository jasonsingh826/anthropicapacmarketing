Measurement

Two outcomes. Market position and revenue contribution.

The function is accountable for whether the region's regard for Anthropic moves, and whether that regard converts. Both are measured, both are reported, and one is not permitted to stand in for the other.

Reporting should explain performance rather than describe activity. Beneath each outcome sit five signals, and beneath those the metrics each programme produces, so that when a number moves the cause is identifiable and the response is specific rather than simply more spend.

Using it

A miss should be diagnosable, not mysterious.

Pipeline soft this quarter

Check progression before creation. A coverage problem and a volume problem look identical on a pipeline report and need opposite responses.

Coverage thin in an account

The ladder names which persona is missing, which points at one specific programme rather than at more activity.

Brand flat, revenue healthy

Capture is running on demand someone else created. The creation side is under-fed, and it will surface as a revenue problem about two quarters later.

Two things worth saying out loud

Brand measures move slowly and will not show a clean result inside a first year, which is why the baseline reading matters more than the first movement. And in a consumption business, attribution to marketing is genuinely partial. The honest claim is contribution, not credit.
