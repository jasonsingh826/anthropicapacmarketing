The programmes

Concentration, not coverage.

Fifteen programmes, each accountable to a named seat in the buying committee and a defined commercial outcome. Where a programme cannot name both, it does not run. Three verticals are funded properly rather than six funded thinly.

That is a real finding rather than a bug. Where a persona has no programme at a given mindset, either the seat is reached indirectly through another programme, or there is a genuine gap to fund once headcount allows.

Where the programmes point

Three verticals funded. Three reached another way.

A team of one cannot run six verticals. Naming all six as priorities would be the tell that the job has not actually been done.

Tier 1 · funded

Financial services

Regulated, high willingness to pay, strong across Japan, Singapore, Australia and Korea. Trust and assurance programmes carry most of the weight.

Tier 1 · funded

Retail and commerce

Customer service and commerce operations are already instrumented. Handle time, deflection and cost per contact exist before Claude arrives, so the before number is already there. Fastest route to a defensible outcome.

Tier 1 · funded

Technology and software

Startups and ISVs building on the API. The warmest ground in the region and the shortest path from builder credibility to commercial expansion.

Tier 2 · reached indirectly

Public sector

Earns its place through the government and regulatory programme rather than vertical demand generation.

Tier 2 · reached indirectly

Professional services

This is the channel rather than the target. Covered by partner co-marketing, where they are the vehicle carrying Anthropic into accounts.

Tier 2 · reached indirectly

Telco and media

Concentrated enough in Korea and Japan to run account-based rather than as a vertical campaign.
