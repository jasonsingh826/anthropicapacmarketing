Jason Singh · APAC Field Marketing · Anthropic

The best marketing systems outlive the marketer who built them.

I have built enterprise marketing functions from nothing five times, across four companies. What follows is the system I would build for Anthropic in Asia Pacific.

Over fifteen years I have joined Telstra, AWS, Rackspace, Workplace from Meta and Meta's business AI products as the first marketing hire into a region or a function. Each of those roles started with no team, no programmes, no operating model and no way of proving anything worked. Each ended with all four in place and generating pipeline the business could count on.

I then moved to New York and spent seven years leading global partner and field marketing at Meta, running a team of fifteen and a programme budget that grew roughly tenfold, from $2.1 million. After seven years of building from a distance, I am moving back to Singapore permanently, and I intend to spend the next decade of my career building in this region rather than describing it to people who are not here.

I have accepted a role leading partner marketing for APJ at OpenAI. I am putting my name forward here because this remit is the broader one. Field marketing is the discipline I have spent my career in, and partner marketing is one mechanism inside it rather than the whole of it.

This is not a list of tactics. Anyone can produce one of those, and it stops being useful the moment conditions change. What I build instead are systems: a way of reading the market, a way of deciding who to talk to and what to say, a portfolio with a funding rule, and a measurement architecture that makes performance explainable rather than merely reportable.

Four systems, set out over the next four pages, together with the budget and resourcing model that follows from them. Each is something I have built before and would build again here.
