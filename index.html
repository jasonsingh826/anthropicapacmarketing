Jason Singh · Singapore

Built from zero in APAC five times.

Five consecutive roles as the first marketing hire in a region or function, across Meta, AWS, Rackspace and Telstra. Each began with no team, no programmes, no operating model and no measurement, and each ended with all four in place and producing pipeline.

I then relocated to the United States to lead global partner and field marketing at Meta, running a team of fifteen and a budget that grew from $2.1M to over $20M. I am now returning to Singapore.

I have recently accepted a role leading partner marketing for APJ at OpenAI. I am here because this remit is the broader one, and closer to where my work has always sat. Field marketing is the primary discipline, with partner marketing as one mechanism inside it rather than the whole of it.

What follows is a plan for Anthropic in APAC. It is built from programmes I have run rather than assembled for an application, which is why the precedent for each one is set out below.

Precedent

Every programme in this plan has been run before.

The programme families on the Programmes page are not categories invented for this document. Each maps to work already delivered, at scale, in this region and globally.

Partner co-marketing 6 to 24 partners · $20M+ revenue lift

Built the High Touch Partner Co-Marketing programme at Meta, quadrupling the partner base and generating over $20M in incremental revenue. MDF-driven pipeline doubled from $23M to $49M across the same period.

Building a region from nothing 12% to 81% engagement · 12 countries

Lifted active partner engagement from 12% to 81% across twelve countries. This is the closest precedent to the Anthropic mandate: no existing programme, multiple national markets, partner-led distribution as the primary mechanism.

Convening and executive hospitality $46M pipeline · 32 races

Designed and ran a Formula 1 hospitality programme across 32 races, generating $46M in pipeline from Fortune 500 executives. Premium venues, curated guest lists, measured on opportunities rather than attendance.

Account-based programmes $45M pipeline · 300 accounts

Built and ran ABM across 300 named accounts producing $45M in pipeline. An earlier programme at Workplace from Meta delivered $5M in pipeline at a 35% MQL to SQO conversion rate.

Community and certification 4,000+ certified · NPS +12 to +67

Certified over four thousand partners and rebuilt the enablement experience, moving NPS from +12 to +67. Recruited and onboarded more than 3,000 ISVs into the ecosystem.

Measurement Built with Data Science, adopted globally

Designed the Meta Partner Marketing Measurement Framework in partnership with Data Science, giving the function a defensible line from programme activity to revenue contribution. The ladder on the Measurement page is the same discipline applied here.

Why this matters for a team of one

A founding hire has no time to learn the job while doing it. Everything in this plan is a repeat rather than an experiment, which is what makes a twelve month timeline credible instead of optimistic.
