System two · the three-layer targeting model

Three decisions specify any asset we make.

The vertical decides which workload we lead with. The persona decides which seat we are addressing, and therefore what the proof has to look like. The horizontal message decides what the asset must establish regardless of either.

Change any one of the three and the asset changes. A financial services roundtable for chief information security officers leads with client onboarding as the workload, addresses the risk seat, and carries security and trust as its horizontal. Move the same event to retail and every one of those decisions moves with it.

The discipline this imposes is worth more than the categories themselves. Anything that cannot name its vertical, its persona and its horizontal has not been thought through, and does not get funded.

Layer three · horizontal messages

Three messages. Every asset carries at least one.

Vertical messaging changes by audience. These do not. The discipline is that there are three of them rather than nine, because a message that everyone is required to carry only works if there are few enough to remember.

Security and trust

The message
Safety posture is procurement evidence rather than a values statement. Anthropic can be adopted inside a regulated institution without the risk function having to take anything on faith.
It must answer
Where data goes and who can see it. Residency and sovereignty by market. Model behaviour under adversarial use. Independent validation. Standing with regulators.
Who for
CISO, CIO, Procurement, and the regulator standing behind them.
Failure mode
Delivering it as philosophy. In this region it only lands as documentation and precedent.

ROI and outcomes

The message
We help you construct the business case in the form your finance function accepts, and we agree the metric before the pilot rather than arguing about it afterwards.
It must answer
What to baseline and when. How returns are attributed when the gain arrives as time rather than cost. What comparable organisations achieved, and how they measured it.
Who for
The line of business leader first and the CFO second. The order matters, because the number the CFO needs is produced by the operator.
Failure mode
Asserting a return. The credible position is helping them prove their own.

Partnership

The message
This is a long-term relationship with a company that will still be here, with people in the region and an ecosystem that can implement and support what you buy.
It must answer
Who implements this locally, who supports it, what access we get to the roadmap, and what happens when the model changes underneath us.
Who for
CEO and Board, CIO, Procurement.
Failure mode
Claiming commitment without local evidence. Offices, partners and named regional customers are the proof.
