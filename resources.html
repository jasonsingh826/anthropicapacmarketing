Resources · first twelve months

Money and headcount, allocated by what has to be proved first.

The allocation below is expressed as percentages, because the split is the judgement and the absolute number is an input. It is modelled on a working assumption of $4M for year one. Change the number and the shape holds.

It is deliberately weighted toward credibility. In a market where proof does not travel, there is a ceiling on how much demand capture can convert until local proof exists to convert it with.

Restraint

What I would not fund in year one.

For a team of one, what gets declined matters more than what gets approved.

Large-format trade show booths

High cost, badge-scan measurement, and they reach the wrong seat. The hospitality and curated-meeting model at the same events delivers more against open pipeline for a fraction of the spend.

Regional brand advertising

Expensive, slow to read, and it solves an awareness problem that does not exist here. Anthropic is known. It is not yet believed on enterprise deployment, which is a proof problem rather than a reach problem.

A second flagship event

One owned APAC moment done properly beats two done adequately. The second one gets funded when the first has a measured result behind it.

Broad content localisation

Translating the full library is a large bill with weak returns. Localise only what the funded verticals actually use in live deals, and let usage decide what gets translated next.

Headcount

Team of one, then three.

Each hire is triggered by evidence rather than by calendar. The sequence follows where the leverage is, and partner-led distribution is the primary mechanism, so that seat comes first.

Q1

One, plus a contractor bench

No hires. Event execution and design bought in rather than built. The quarter goes on baselining the brand study, auditing partner coverage, and sitting in on live deals to find where they actually stall.

First in-market hire

Japan or Korea, decided by where pipeline concentrated over the preceding three quarters rather than by a view formed on day one. In-language and in-market, because that is what the proof requires.

Headcount 4

The constraint that shapes all of it

Until Q2 there is one person and a budget. That is why the allocation buys leverage rather than activity: partners who run programmes without us in the room, agencies who execute rather than advise, and proof assets that get reused across every market instead of being built once and retired.
