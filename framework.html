The APAC opportunity

APAC enterprise buyers buy on trust, credibility, and partnership.

Enterprise AI is a long-term bet on a company rather than a feature comparison, and it is largely settled before a vendor conversation starts.

In this region those signals are local. Japanese buying runs on consensus, Korean enterprises follow precedent set at home, Australian public sector wants regulatory standing before product, and Indian buyers want the economics proven at their own volume. Much of the buying runs through partners. APAC is five buying cultures rather than one region, and the proof has to be built inside each one.

The evaluating quarter is the opportunity. They are deciding now, on trust they can verify locally.

01 · The buyer layer

Nobody in APAC is unaware of AI.

The classic top of the awareness cycle is empty. Enterprises here have already bought, already deployed, and mostly cannot prove what they got. The unawareness sits one level down, and it is about what enterprise adoption actually requires.

03 · Why run both

An account can sit at most aware and still be blocked at credibility, because the CFO does not believe the number.

No amount of capture activity fixes that. Discounting will not fix it either. The account looks late-stage on every dashboard and will not close, and the reason is invisible if you only run one layer.

Awareness tells you what to say. Stage tells you what to prove. Reading them together turns a stalled deal from a mystery into a diagnosis, and it points at a specific programme rather than more activity.

Two programmes carry the loop

Customer proof is the only programme that operates at every mindset, because a named local outcome works on all six. Community and certification sits at post-close and feeds straight back into customer proof at problem aware. That return path is what makes this a loop rather than a funnel, and it is the main thing the function manufactures.
